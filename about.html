We’re a news channel founded in 1990 by livE Co's Miscommunication Division totally-a-real-news-anchor ltd. — a partnership that definitely wasn’t a clerical accident. Since our creation, we’ve specialized in delivering groundbreaking reports that break absolutely nothing, investigations that investigate very little, and journalism that boldly tiptoes where no real journalist would ever bother going. Over the decades, we’ve mastered the fine art of misunderstanding events, misquoting sources, and occasionally misplacing entire stories. Our dedicated team of almost-professional writers, accidental reporters, and enthusiastic button-pressers work tirelessly (or at least work) to bring you the finest selection of fake news, satire, and stories that somehow get more unbelievable the longer you read them. At Genuine Fake News, we’re not just reporting the news — we’re reinventing it, rewriting it, and sometimes completely making it up on the spot. And honestly? We think that’s a public service.
